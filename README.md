# AdventureWorks-HR-SQL-Project
📊 **Project Overview**

This repository contains 15 structured SQL queries designed to extract strategic workforce insights from the AdventureWorks2019 database (specifically focusing on the HumanResources and Person schemas). 
The queries are built to answer critical business questions regarding headcount distribution, payroll expenditure, employee demographics, recruitment conversion rates, and organizational hierarchy.

🗄️ **Database Schema Covered**

•	HumanResources_Department: Organizational units and group names.

•	HumanResources_Employee: Employee job titles, hire dates, birth dates, and demographics.

•	HumanResources_EmployeeDepartmentHistory: Departmental assignments and active employment timelines.

•	HumanResources_EmployeePayHistory: Compensation rates and pay frequencies.

•	HumanResources_Shift: Work shift schedules.

•	HumanResources_JobCandidate: Applicant tracking and recruitment pipelines.

•	Person_Person & Person_Address: Employee names, geographic locations, and contact details.


📋 **Key Business Questions Addressed**

1.	Current Active Headcount by Department
2.	Average Pay Rate by Department
3.	Employee Distribution by Work Shift
4.	Gender Breakdown across Job Roles
5.	Top 10 Longest-Serving Active Employees
6.	Recent Hires Tracking
7.	Employee Pay History and Rate Progression
8.	Geographic Distribution of Employees
9.	Job Candidate Application and Hiring Conversion
10.	Estimated Annual Payroll Cost per Department
11.	Internal Mobility (Employees with Multiple Department Histories)
12.	Employee Contact Details Audit
13.	Demographics: Average Age by Marital Status and Gender
14.	Salaried vs. Hourly Workforce Breakdown
15.	Management Hierarchy Depth
    
🚀 **How to Use**

1.	Ensure you have Microsoft SQL Server and SQL Server Management Studio (SSMS) installed.
2.	Restore the AdventureWorks2019 database on your local SQL Server instance.
3.	Open any script from the queries/ folder and execute it against the database.
